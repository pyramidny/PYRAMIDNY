The life of a job — start to finish

One picture of everything that happens from a client's first call to a paid, closed project. Read it top to bottom. At each step, three columns tell you what happens in three different places — and those places are not the same thing.

In the app

Who works it. The portal — where people enter and see information. Separation between crews lives here, as views and permissions.

In SharePoint

Where files live. One folder per job, holds the documents. Passive storage — it doesn't separate anyone.

In QuickBooks

The money. Invoices, payments, aging. QuickBooks does the math and stays the source of truth for dollars.

Constant the whole way: the job's number and its SharePoint folder are created once, at bid time, and never change or move. Only ownership and stage change as the job progresses.

1

Client comes in

Admin / PM— prospect —
A management company or owner is entered. Starts as a prospect until they award work.
AppCreate the client in the Clients area. Marked prospect.
SharePointNothing yet — no folder until there's a bid.
2

Job sites & contacts added

Admin / PM
Each building the client manages, plus the people — office contacts on the client, site contacts on the buildings. One person can cover several buildings.
AppAdd job sites (BIN, block, lot) and contacts. Flag one as the billing contact.
SharePointStill nothing — sites and contacts are data, not files.
3

Bid is logged

Bidding crewStage 1
An estimator opens a bid on a job site. This is the moment the job gets its number and its folder — both permanent.
AppBid created in the Bids workspace. Number assigned: A11557 (IRA) or P11558 (Regular), one shared sequence.
SharePointFolder created and named with that number: A11557 — 515 W 23rd St/. Bid document goes into 1. Estimating Phase/.
4

Bid is worked

Bidding crewStages 1–2
Pricing, submission, interview, follow-up. The bidding crew owns it entirely — production isn't involved yet.
AppLives in the Bids workspace. Estimators only. Billing hears nothing.
SharePointEstimating folder fills with pricing, drawings, walkthrough notes.
↳ If lost: marked lost with a reason, kept for win-rate reporting. The job stops here.
5

Bid is won — the handoff

Bidding→ ProductionStage 3 · Awarded
The one moment everything pivots. Ownership passes from bidding to production, the client becomes a real client, and Billing is notified — all automatically. Nothing physically moves.
AppProject leaves the Bids workspace and appears in the Production view. Client promotes prospect → client. A billing-queue item is created.
SharePointSame folder — nothing moves. 2. Production/ and 3. Photos/ now become the active areas; bid docs stay in Estimating as the record.
6

Kickoff meeting

PM · Billing · Admin
Job details, insurance and permit requirements, scope, and payment schedule are walked through before work is scheduled — normally a 2–3 month runway before production starts. This meeting stays exactly as it is today; the app just has everything ready for it.
AppThe bid doc, insurance and permit requirements, scope and payment schedule are already on the project — nothing to gather first.
SharePointBid and reference docs are in the Estimating folder, linked from the project.
7

Billing sets up the job

Billing · Noemi
The billing queue hands Noemi everything she needs, so she never hunts for it.
AppQueue card shows client, contract, bill-to inline, plus a one-click link to the bid doc.
QuickBooksNoemi creates the Customer:Job and pastes the path back into the app. That path is the key every invoice matches on.
8

Production runs

Production crewStages 4–6
Pre-con, mobilization, the actual work. PMs and field own it now.
AppLives in the Regular / IRA project views. PM, APM, field crew. Stages advance as work progresses.
SharePointProduction and Photos folders fill — field reports, daily logs, progress photos.
QuickBooksNoemi invoices and records payments as the work bills out — including AIA pay applications on larger jobs.
9

Money comes back into view

Billing → everyone
The A/R loop — how the team sees what's paid without asking Finance.
QuickBooksNoemi runs the memorized A/R Aging Detail report and exports it to CSV or Excel (not PDF, so the app can read it).
AppThe file is uploaded; the app matches each invoice to its project by Customer:Job and shows paid / unpaid / 30-60-90 aging right on the job. The Rep / PM on each invoice is tracked and routes its A/R to that PM's view — automating the per-PM tabs Finance keeps by hand.
10

Closeout

Production · BillingStages 7–10
Final payment, closeout package, retainage released, marked closed.
AppFinal stages advance to Closed. The full history stays on the project.
SharePointCloseout documents filed. The one folder now holds the whole job, bid to close.
