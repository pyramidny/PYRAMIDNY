The pivot — billing today vs. the suggested change

Read each row across: how it works today on the left, the suggested change on the right. The goal isn't to change how you run a job or how you invoice — it's to stop the hunting. Nothing here is final; this is the starting point for your input.

What is NOT changing

The kickoff meeting stays exactly as it is. Job details, insurance and permit requirements, scope, payment schedule — that meeting is how you set a job up right, and the app doesn't replace it. QuickBooks stays your source of truth for money — the app never does the math or writes dollars into QuickBooks. And you still invoice the way you do today. We're only changing where the documents and the status come from.

The timeline stays the same too. There's normally 2–3 months between award and the start of scheduling. The app just holds everything — bid docs, insurance and permit requirements — ready and in one place during that window, instead of it living in people's heads and folders.

4

Billing labels & invoice format

Billing comes up with the labels and formats the invoice per location — improvised each time. That's why the same client's jobs get named inconsistently in QuickBooks.

Improvised · inconsistent
→

Billing creates the Customer:Job once and pastes the exact path back into the app. From then on it's recorded and reused — every invoice for that job matches automatically.

Recorded once · consistent
5

A/R status to management

Billing generates A/R reports from time to time and shares them as a static PDF to keep management updated — a snapshot nothing can be done with, and management has to hunt for the info when they want the current picture.

Static PDF · hunt for status
→

Billing runs the same memorized report and exports it to CSV or Excel — not a PDF, which is what lets the app read it. Uploaded once, the app matches each invoice to its job and shows paid / unpaid / 30-60-90 aging live.

The Rep / PM on each invoice routes it to that PM's view — the same per-PM split Finance keeps by hand today, done automatically. No more chasing Billing for status.

CSV / Excel · live · auto-routed to PM
+

New tracking point — Rep / PM on every invoice

Today the Rep / PM is tracked by keeping separate tabs per PM in the export by hand — one person maintaining six views every time the report runs.

Maintained manually
→

The Rep / PM already on every QuickBooks invoice is carried into the app as a tracked field, so each job's A/R lands in the right PM's view on its own. If a PM ever changes on a job, it's one update, not a re-sorted spreadsheet.

Tracked field · no manual sorting
